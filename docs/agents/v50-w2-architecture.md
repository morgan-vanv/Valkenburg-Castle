Kill dev servers by PID, never `pkill -f`.

What W0 built: bevelled hex tiles with gaps on every level (`bevelGeometry`, `tileJitter`, a dark under-tile); brick runs on five line families (three hex axes, two vertical zigzags) with the slab face at 0.45 / 0.433 from the centreline, plinths in the notches, pillars at run ends and for lone hexes, `LookWalls.frame()` and `sconceAt()` for things that hang on a face; gated to the Great Hall and dais by `LOOK_WALL_ROOMS`.

1. **Open the brick walls to every room** (`LOOK_WALL_ROOMS` empty = all), then walk all three levels with the shot script and fix what breaks: cave rooms (rock, earth, bone materials) get dark rock mass instead of brick (a rough hex block, jittered, no running bond); wall hexes away from any floor stay solid blocks; T-junctions and corners where three runs meet (one pillar, no gaps); runs that end at a door get jamb pillars and a lintel over the door; the sealed door and the barred gate keep their meaning (the barred gate still draws as an ordinary door: fix that while you're there).
2. **Matching pieces in the new style**: cracked walls (a brick run with a visible fissure and loose bricks), rubble heaps, built walls (unbreakable: a cleaner, lighter course), doors (planks in the brick jamb; locked ones with the padlock), stairs, pits (the ledge skirt and piers under the bevelled tiles; check the pit lip reads as a drop), railings, raised floors (a taller bevelled tile), bridges, the lift. Fixed lights: sconces hang on the brick face (W0's `sconceAt`), braziers in the new style. What blocks movement must read as blocking at a glance; no walkable hex may be covered.
3. **The cutaway** (`settings.cutaway`, today off): with the lower camera the near wall hides the floor behind it. Make it smarter and on by default: cut walls on the camera's side of the focus to a stub (the plinth height), keep the far side full; the instanced bricks and cores already carry the `cutaway` fog option (`CUTAWAY` uniforms in `src/view/fog.ts`), so this is mostly tuning the shader's window and moving the plinths out of the cut. Keep the see-through hero silhouettes.
4. **Dressing table for the rest of Level 1** only if time allows (W4 does all 33 rooms): the pattern is in `dressing.ts` (a placement names a wall hex in offset coordinates and the direction it faces; pieces on plinths are skipped where the notch is too shallow).
