# Brief: V3.7 crews that fight back (engine)

Read docs/agents/v37-common.md, then docs/v3-spec.md 20.4 in full, plus 18.5–18.6 (crews and parley as they are), 20.1–20.3 (stealth, swings and fused charges, now in the engine: read docs/agents/v37-stealth-progress.md and v37-sapper-progress.md). You own src/game/rivals.ts, parley.ts, a new src/game/crewai.ts (item use and focus fire), camp.ts (captives, hideout contracts), data.ts (crew tables), the capture/robbery hooks in world-phase.ts and delve.ts, src/data/castle/index.ts (hideout sites), tests under `describe('V3.7 crews')`, and scripts/v3-engine-sim.ts.

1. **Crew builder:** `makeCrew`/`buildCrew` build members as heroes of their class at the crew's level (CLASSES table + 2 HP and +5 main stat per level above 1), `level`, `weaponTier`, `armorTier`, `pack` per the 20.4 table; `rivalStats` uses them (weapon tier +10 per slot). The Wake (level 4) on L3 contracts at 60%, posts on L3 (`rivalPosts` per level: add L3 posts near the lift base and the Lair approach). Keep Rival.cls values; add 'hedgewitch' to RivalClass only if the contract allows (it doesn't: request it, and build the Wake's healer as a shadowmancer with Mend-like behaviour, or wait for the lead: request first, work around second).
2. **Item use and spells** (crewai.ts): per-class rules from 20.4 with intents shown the round before (`item` with the item kind in `Intent`, `potion`, existing `dart`/`smoke`/`fuse`/`firebolt`/`overwatch`/`veil`); members use the heroes' own item rules (reuse delve.ts item functions where exported; export what you need). Potions below half HP after backing off one hex. Hired members use the pack for the party.
3. **Focus fire** and positioning: one target per round (lantern bearer in reach, else lowest HP), members path to hexes with line of sight to the target, preferring hexes not in sight of other heroes.
4. **Captives:** robbery (`robbed`, gold and gems to the member's loot) and dragging (`dragging`, 3 a round toward the crew's exit; intent `drag`/`rob`), freed when the dragger is killed, downed or feared; `captured` at the exit; hero status `captured`; `backToCamp` moves the hero to `campaign.captives` (minus gold and gems), adds a "Bring back <name>" rescue contract (full bounty for the level below, site = the crew's hideout, one `ContractSiteDef` per crew with `story`-like exclusion: use a new `hideout: CrewId` field if the contract allows; else reuse `story` with a cast and request the field); the freed hero rejoins at the HP they come out with. Captured Keven ends the campaign ("The heir is taken. Valkenburg stays dark.").
5. **Spoils:** killed members drop pack and purse (30 at level 2–3, 50 at level 4).
6. **Sim:** fight the crew on 1 in 3 crew delves; report crew fights won/lost, heroes captured, rescues done; tune toward the balance target in 20.4 (change crew levels/tiers, not hero rules) and write the numbers you settled on in the spec table's terms in your progress note.
7. Tests for each rule. Keep vitest green; coordinate with the presentation agent (they're building the UI for this against fixtures) through docs/agents/v37-crews-progress.md.
