1. **Action cam** as one module, `src/view/look/actioncam.ts`, with one call: `dio.actionCam(actorId, targetId, ms)`. On every attack, blow, shot, spell and item use (the party's and the castle's), and on enemy moves as today's follow: push in about 30% closer and slightly lower toward the midpoint of actor and target, hold while the event's existing animation plays, then ease back to exactly where the player had the camera (target, yaw, pitch bias, distance). Never adds time (pillar 2): it fits inside the animation; skipped at the fastest speed (`clock.speed` ≥ 6) and when `reduceShake`. If the player moves the camera during it, the player wins (any rig input cancels the return). The action cam hands the camera back to wherever the play track's follow and selection rules leave it: do not touch `autoAdvance` or follow in app.ts/hud.ts. Settings (append one line each to `src/ui/settings.ts`): `actionCamera` (default on), `tiltShift` (default on); the play track adds the menu rows, so expose `dio.setActionCamera(on)` and `dio.setTiltShift(on)`.
