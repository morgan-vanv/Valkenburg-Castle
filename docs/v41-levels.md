# V4.1 levels: Level 1 teaches, Level 2 tests (WS8, for owner review)

Owner (2026-10-26): every core mechanic is taught, or at least shown in play, on Level 1, and every one is tested in some way on Level 2. Darkness is Level 2's first check, but sound, stealth, positioning, items and class utility must not be neglected. This note lists what each level already does and the changes proposed (marked **Change**). Numbers and rules for light, poison and carrying come from docs/v41-plan.md (WS2, WS3).

## Level 1, the Ruined Keep: the classroom

| Mechanic | Where it's shown | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sneaking past sleepers, backstab | Kitchens rats (Wim's rescue), the library spider | **L1-1** On a campaign's first delve the chapel orc guarding Aldric sleeps (WS1, B1): the first lesson is a sneak and a backstab, not a brawl. |
| Patrols, sight by mood | The hall's patrolling orc round the pit mouth | none |
| Light | Most of the level is daylit | **L1-2** The library stacks, the armory and the tower top go dark (WS2). The tower and armory chests (tier 2, the richest on the level) sit in the dark: a torch pays for itself, and a party sees how short unlit sight is before it matters. |
| Sound as a tool | Walking noise and the orc's bellow | **L1-3** The tower stair (the way down to Level 2) gets a second posted orc. Two awake orcs make the stair a costly fight; a firecracker thrown into the hall (or a whistling arrow) sends both searching and opens the stair. The lure has an obvious, rewarding use on the level that sells firecrackers. |
| Poison | The library spider's bite | Poison never kills (WS3), so a novice survives the lesson; the antipoison and Mend show their worth. |
| Locks and keys | The armory door and chest; the sally port key in the library | none |
| Traps | The servants' passage, the gate passage | **L1-4** The servants' passage trap sits in a dim stretch (no daylight on that hex): in the dark the Wits roll to spot it is −20, so a lit party spots it more often. A first taste of "light helps you see danger". |
| Terrain | The hall's pit mouth | **L1-5** The hall's west orc is posted on the pit's unrailed lip (moved a hex or two if needed): a shove, knockback or gust drops it to Level 2 (the kill counts). |
| Items | Mott's wagon | Every Level 1 item has a use here: torch (dark rooms), antipoison (spider), rope (the pit mouth is a shortcut to the Crossing), firecracker (L1-3), blasting charge (the armory lock, the breach, the cracked wall), plain arrows. |
| Class utility | | Burglar: the armory lock and the sneak into the library. Sapper: the east breach rubble and the armory–chapel cracked wall. Ranger: the courtyard's long sight lines. Shadowmancer: Sleep on the hall patrol. Pyromancer: the hall's orcs bunch. Hedgewitch: Gust at the pit lip (L1-5). |
| Crews and parley | The Lantern Sisters (neutral, carrying torches: WS2) | none: a fight with them is a choice. |
| Carrying and shackles | Level 1 rescue sites | Prisoners are shackled (WS3): a Burglar's picks or Mott's key free them; without, you carry them out slowly. |

## Level 2, the Crossing: the check

| Mechanic tested | How | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Darkness | Bridges, arcade and ledge are dark; four sconces, the forge fire and the pit glow | With no free lantern (WS2), an unlit hero sees 2 hexes and rolls at −20. A party without light is half blind on the bridges, under archers. |
| Stealth and noise discipline | The barracks squad (four orcs and the captain) sleeps beside the arcade; the copper is heavy (move 3) and sits among them | **L2-1** Ugluk Red-Hand (berserker, Gerda's forge) starts **asleep** at the forge fire. Today he is awake and the forge rescue is a straight slugfest (the sim's Level 2 wall); asleep, Gerda's rescue becomes a stealth test that punishes noise: a careless walk wakes him and he never stops hunting. |
| Positioning and sight lines | Two archers above the pit; patrols on the arcade loop and the east ledge | **L2-2** The two archer perches become raised floor (+10 to ranged attacks from them; the rule exists, no hex used it). Heroes who take a perch get the same edge. Approaching under archers rewards cover and timing; taking their perch rewards a plan. |
| Sound as a tool | The winch (noise 6) in the winch room by the cellblock | **L2-3** Grub the Jailer guards the cellblock door from the winch room side, so working the winch (needed for the lift) draws him; a lure first (a firecracker into the arcade, a whistling arrow) or a quiet kill solves it. Tests what L1-3 taught. |
| Items | | Smoke breaks the archers' line of sight; glue holds the arcade patrol on a bridge; rope and the grappling hook use the pit; antipoison or Mend for the two poison traps (which sit in dark, 1-wide corridors: light helps spot them). |
| Class utility | | Burglar: the officers' locked door and strongbox, shackled prisoners in the cellblock. Sapper: the officers'–archive cracked wall shortcut, blowing the forge approach. Ranger: counter-sniping the archers, a rope arrow over the pit. Shadowmancer: Sleep on Ugluk if he wakes, Blink across a bridge. Hedgewitch: Gust a patrol off a bridge. |
| Crews | The Ferrymen or the Gallows Choir at a post, as an equal power (WS7) | Fought only with intent: a battle-worn party should look for parley or another way. |
| Carrying | The copper (heavy), captives in the cells | Carrying a shackled captive (move 3) past the arcade is a test of planning; unshackling first is the skilled answer. |

## Not changing

Level 3 (the Hollow) keeps its layout; the Gloam now hunts only unlit heroes there (WS2).

## Checks

castle.test keeps its invariants (paths, sites, ways). New invariants: the tower stair's two posts are within the hall's firecracker reach; the archer perches are raised and adjacent to floor; Ugluk's hex sees the forge rescue site; the servants' trap hex is unlit.
