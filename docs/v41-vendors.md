# V4.1 vendors: the full catalog (WS4, WS5, WS6)

Everything any vendor in Lowgate will ever sell, with its gate and the hint shown while it's locked. The Outfit screen's "Vendors" side lists all of it (playtest setting `SHOW_LOCKED = true`); locked rows are dimmed, show a lock and no Buy button, and their hover gives the hint instead of the rule. Hints are Keven's thoughts or a villager's word, never a rule or a map marker (pillar 11: don't spoonfeed). Prices in gold. "For a hero" means bought for the selected hero (gear, ales).
