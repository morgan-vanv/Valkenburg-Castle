# Decisions

A running log. Newest at the bottom. Later entries override earlier ones where they disagree (the V1 and V2 entries at the top are mostly superseded); `docs/current-rules.md` has the live picture.

- **2026-10-23** Static site on GitHub Pages, TypeScript and Vite. Keeps the demo free to host with no server to maintain.
- **2026-10-23** Rules engine kept separate from rendering. Lets us test the rules and try different visual directions independently.
- **2026-10-23** All randomness seeded. Runs can be replayed and bugs reproduced from a seed.
- **2026-10-23** One roguelite run is one campaign of several weeks. Closest to the 1980 siege structure; expeditions are the campaign's turns.
- **2026-10-23** The castle is generated once per campaign and fixed for that campaign, with the 1980 map as a classic seed. Keeps the feel of retaking one place while staying replayable.
- **2026-10-23** Multi-man squads fight alongside named heroes. It is the 1980 game's identity and what makes frontage matter.
- **2026-10-23** Desktop first with mouse and keyboard; UI kept touch-friendly to adapt later. Lets the camera and UI be designed without phone compromises.
- **2026-10-23** Demo economy tuned: goal 2,000 gold, outfitting 100, searches find something on 3–6, deeper levels add more to treasure rolls. The 1980 tables assume a 52-week siege; at 4 weeks a simple bot banked almost nothing.
- **2026-10-23** V2: one-shot run instead of a 4-week campaign; a new spacious two-level map instead of the 1980 transcription; doors, loot and carrying simplified (see docs/archive/v2-changes.md). Play-testers found V1 claustrophobic and clunky; V1 is kept on the `v1-prototype` branch.

The full design lives in the Game Design Document: https://claude.ai/artifact/5p4XddApJVqAodPrEuEGzc
- **2026-10-24** V3: rebuilt as a turn-based extraction crawler (Dark and Darker premise, For the King 2 slot rolls, XCOM permadeath) on a new two-level hex castle; light is the clock, noise is spatial. The owner found the 1980-derived rules encumbering; see docs/v3-changes.md.
- **2026-10-24** V3 rounds are a player phase then a world phase, with no initiative order. Faster to read and play; enemy intents carry the information.
- **2026-10-24** V3 lantern is 40 rounds. With 60 a bot finished every contract with most of its light left.

The V3 Game Design Document: https://claude.ai/artifact/Q7766w5bBz11paXkw4YPf1
- **2026-10-24** Exploration reveals layout by line of sight from every step, lit or not; units still need light. The owner found long moves left the map full of holes.
- **2026-10-24** V3.1: three-level castle from the approved map proposal (pass 2); falls go straight down pit columns; the Dragon is the demo's final boss. The owner wanted verticality, asymmetry and terrain tactics.
- **2026-10-24** The Hollow's cave mouth is open from the start. Carrying the hoard the length of the Hollow is the cost; a locked-until-found shortcut wasn't worth the extra rules for the demo.
- **2026-10-24** Design pillar: visual clarity, with intention. Once seen, important things (stairs, objectives, interactables, edges) announce themselves and what you can do with them; markers never appear for unexplored hexes. Driven by play-test: the player couldn't find how to take stairs or why the heirloom wouldn't pick up.
- **2026-10-24** V3.2 line of sight is permissive: a hex is seen if a line to its center or to any corner (pulled 10% in) is clear, and solid hexes next to seen floor are seen too. The strict center-to-center line left holes beside pillars and walls (owner's screenshot).
- **2026-10-24** V3.2 dash: a hero who has moved and not acted may move again for their action. Acting first keeps a plain move; climb, jump and grapple still take the whole turn.
- **2026-10-24** V3.2 a Veil breaks on any attack or push the hero makes (including fireball and gust) or any action of noise 4+; Sleep and the sleep dart are silent and don't break it. Ward reduces monster and Gloam hits only, never falls, fire or breath.
- **2026-10-24** V3.2 `take` is never silent: an option that can't be done now is returned with a plain-words reason (`ActionOption.blocked`), the contract item is taken first, and the preview names exactly what will be taken.
- **2026-10-24** V3.2 the Burglar opens chests without spending the action. The spec's "takes and opens chests silently" added nothing to a take that was already free and silent, so the class got a real perk instead.
- **2026-10-24** Take stairs/lift is hotkey T and wins over Torch's T on that hex (no free letter left; torch is rare while standing on stairs).
- **2026-10-24** End turn asks once when any hero still has a move or action; a settings checkbox turns it off. Scripts turn it off through `vc.settings.noEndTurnAsk`.
- **2026-10-24** Interactable hover = ember ring; hazard (pit, trap) = red ring; floor = parchment. Tooltip form is "Thing · what you can do".
- **2026-10-24** Beacon colours by contract: recover gold, rescue green, slay red (diorama BEACON_COLOR and .beacon-tag CSS).
- **2026-10-24** Walls are 2.2 high (hero ≈ 2.6) so thin panels still read as walls; the cutaway keeps the party visible.
- **2026-10-24** Cave = room material rock, earth or bone; those walls stay thick. Water (the Drain) and bone-free built rooms get thin walls.
- **2026-10-24** Figures start facing the camera.
- **2026-10-24** Scale: option C (RuneScape) from the three-panel study; hero 2.0 units, natural proportions, walls 2.6. The owner picked it as good enough for now and wants to revisit.
- **2026-10-24** Scale direction C: a hero is 2.0 world units, walls 2.6, humanoids built parametrically (head 1/5, limb 0.07, shoulders 0.34, legs 0.44) and monsters scaled relative to the hero (unit.ts FIGURE_SCALE). The Dragon is unchanged. Delve camera 19.
- **2026-10-24** Shutter is hotkey L and wins over Light (brazier) and Oil's L for the lantern bearer; L also toggles the lantern when another hero is selected and has no L button (it's free and any-time).
- **2026-10-24** "Start shuttered" is a presentation option: the prepare screen remembers it and the app sends the free `shutter` action right after `startDelve` (no contract change).
- **2026-10-24** The Gloam's creeping darkness is drawn on visible unlit hexes within 2 of the light, not on every dark hex, so it reads as an edge.
- **2026-10-24** Known patrol routes are drawn as straight segments between waypoints (the engine's path between them isn't published); good enough for "where it walks".
- **2026-10-24** V3.3 "fear by depth": monsters awake by default with patrols; lantern 20 with a free shutter; sight per hero (lit: stage range; dark: 4/2/1 by level); the Gloam hunts any unlit hero in the Hollow with no grace; depth tiers; archer, captain, ghoul; the Sisters use the heroes' own kit. The owner found the castle too forgiving and wanted Level 3 to be frightening.
- **2026-10-25** V3.4 economy: start 120 gold, recruits 60 + 40 a level, opening tavern all level 1. The owner wants the first purse to force a choice (two hires, or one hire and cheap gear) so looting and replaying pay.
- **2026-10-25** HP carries over between delves and the infirmary heals 3 gold per HP; the revived-heroes-sit-out rule is dropped, since lost HP is now the cost of getting hurt (one mechanic instead of two).
- **2026-10-25** Far sight shows light sources, not the floor they light (daylight excepted), and sight in a castle light other than daylight is 4 minus depth; your own lantern still gives the stage range. The owner's unlit jump into the Hollow revealed too much; light you carry should matter more than light you happen to stand in.

- **2026-10-25** V3.6: villagers get their own story sites with a reason each (never used by the regular board); only the Burglar picks by default, lockpicks (smith) let anyone; night-eye draught removes dark penalties but not the Gloam; exits marked and buttoned like stairs. Owner's midday play-test.
- **2026-10-25** Approved for V3.7 (owner's answers in https://claude.ai/artifact/CZWDajgUP9WrVr4L39HgXH): stealth rework with monster facing (sneak up on the hex behind), attacks of opportunity for both sides, a Shadowmancer fear spell, the Sapper with fused charges and breakable built walls on L1–L2 (tripwire later, as a smith upgrade), crews built like player squads with full item access, captives and rescue-your-own-hero contracts.
- **2026-10-25** V3.7 stealth engine: a monster with no `facing` watches all round (crew members at their post, old saves); spawned monsters always face somewhere (data, default east). A hero's sneak/walk mode is their last move and persists until the next one, so creeping up and then standing still stays quiet. Monsters remember who they've noticed (`noticedIds`) for sneak attacks and free swings, and forget when they lose their quarry; hostile or demanding crews count as having noticed everyone. Heroes never free-swing at neutral or hired crews walking past.
- **2026-10-25** V3.7 sapper engine: the satchel is one slotless `charge` pack item (`amount` = charges left) so it takes no pack room and bought charges stack beside it; it refills each delve and never reaches the stash. A fuse lit during a world phase (a rival's throw) skips that phase's countdown so the player always sees the marker for a turn. A built wall is breakable when it stands between two floor hexes that aren't neighbours (so notches and the outer curtain never count), is on L1/L2, isn't cave rock or `unbreakable`; broken walls read as rubble (move 2) until dug. The blast shield covers the setter's own side (heroes for a hero's charge, the crew for a rival's); the other side takes full damage. Everyone in the inner ring is thrown back, the setter included.
- **2026-10-25** V3.7 crews engine: members are built from the hero class table (`rivalStats` from `CLASSES` + level + tiers) so a crew is exactly a player squad with more levels; the pack is the source of truth for the kit (`charges` is the mages' mana). A dragged hero does not bleed (the crew keeps them alive to sell) so capture can actually happen; the dragged hero trails one hex behind the dragger and only the member's `moved` event is sent (the view hauls the figure). A hero downed by a member's own blow is robbed on the spot; a downed hero within 2 draws a `rob` intent first. The hideout sites are one level below the crew's own (capped at 3) and the "Bring back" bounty is that level's full rescue bounty; the board rebuilds the contract every camp refresh while the captive is held. A crew's caltrops catch heroes (2 and stop) while the party's own never do (`rivalCaltrops`). The hostile Shadowmancer veils the striker rather than herself; the Sellsword guards the caster (hits meant for an adjacent guarded member land on him).
- **2026-10-25** V3.7 blasting the floor (20.3a): collapse and rubble are decided from the map as it stands before the blast (so one charge never chain-collapses a whole bridge), chest and hoard hexes never give way, loot on collapsing floor falls to the landing hex, and `kindAt` is the single place that turns `collapsed`/`blown` into pit/rubble so sight, movement, shoves, falls and later blasts all agree.
- **2026-10-25** V3.7 kit engine (20.5): calm is computed live (`calmReason`) and mirrored into `Delve.calm` with one `calm` event per change, so hand-built states never carry a stale flag. A calm walk is the mover's steps only: followers walk free, the castle turns on the mover's 4th step even when it is the path's last (4+4+4 walks like 12), and an explicit End turn restarts the count. The walk stops for anything the player would want to react to, but `calm:false` (with the fresh player phase) fires only when calm itself ended; other stops (a sleeper stirring, a trap, a hurt hero, a lantern stage, something newly in sight) are a `say` line and the party keeps its free movement. Roots on heroes tick at the start of the world phase so an archer's hamstring costs two of the hero's turns; roots on monsters and crew members reuse `stuck`. Brace and the shield are applied in one place (`strikeDetailed`) by the attack's label, so every monster and crew strike, free swing, firebolt and overwatch honours them while breath, blasts, falls and the Gloam do not. Smashed props read as floor through `kindAt`, like broken walls.
- **2026-10-25** V3.7.1 (spec 21): the improvised pick is a slotless per-delve kit item like the Sapper's satchel (`isKit`): not dropped, given, banked or counted as lost gear, and a fresh one is issued at every delve start; a hero who picks one up from a fallen Burglar may use it (2 slots). A Burglar with both picks uses the proper ones and keeps the improvised one. The night-eye draught stays a peddler `ShopOffer` (`needs: 'innkeeper'`, bought with `buy`) so no new command was needed; the UI lists it at the peddler only while locked and at the Flagon once Wim is home. Only the noticing ranges moved (`NOTICE`); `MONSTER_SIGHT` (6) and `MONSTER_DARK_SIGHT` (3) still govern what a monster sees once it has noticed someone.
- **2026-10-25** V3.7.1: hunting monsters see as far as they notice a walking hero (sight 8 lit, 5 unlit; was 6/3), so a monster that spots you from 8 hexes doesn't lose you the next phase.
- **2026-10-25** V3.7.2: V3.8 exploration mode reverted and shelved (owner: an improvement but not right yet, more complex than expected; cones are clutter); V3.7's calm free move switched off (`FREE_MOVE.enabled`), so play is entirely turn-based. Code and findings: docs/shelved/exploration-mode.md, branch `shelf/exploration-mode`.
- **2026-10-25** V3.7.2: prospectors never enter within 6 of the party's entrance, come in rounds 6–9, and wait while a hero is within 6 of their way in (owner: the Sisters appeared at once and blocked the exit).
- **2026-10-25** V3.9 engine (spec 24): front and rear halves are decided by `dirIndex` (nearest of the six directions, ties to the lower index), the same rule the view's `dirIndexOf` uses, so markers and rules agree; a figure with no `facing` (old saves, a crew at its post) sees all round. Blindsided (+1 slot) lives in `strikeDetailed` and the hamstring shot, so every slot-rolled enemy attack honours it; free swings are judged before the hero turns for the step (walking straight away from an enemy you face is not blindsided, one already at your back is), and the preview counts the slot. An unseen attacker from behind is revealed (in `visible`) through the end of the next player phase, hit or miss; `unseenHit` fires on hits only. Adjacent enemies the party can't see are not attack/shove targets (turn first; Face is free). Crew Rangers' overwatch also fires only into their front half. Monsters keep tracking heroes they have already noticed by plain sight (they face their quarry anyway).
- **2026-10-25** V3.9 eyes front (spec 24): front-half sight for heroes and enemies, sleepers hear only, free Face, blindsided +1 slot, Burglar quiet feet and backstab (+1 slot, double damage), previews only for seen enemies, free-swing confirm removed; facing shown as arrows only (the translucent sectors read as cones). New tenets: flavor; teach through gameplay; pillar: watch each other's backs.
- **2026-10-25** V3.9.1: the Face command and turning toward an action's target are shelved behind `FACING` flags; heroes face their last step. Owner: err on the side of simplicity before V4.0. Hover rear-half wash removed; facing preview is an arrow.
- **2026-10-26** V3.9.2: actions turn heroes, monsters and crews toward their target again; heroes and crew members are blind only in the cone straight behind (one direction), monsters keep the whole rear half (owner: players and crews the same, monsters more forgiving).
- **2026-10-26** V3.9.3 hearing (spec 24.8): hearing lives in src/game/hearing.ts and only writes `Delve.heard` and `heard` events; it keeps its own copy of the visible set (keyed by a cheap signature of heroes, doors, light, smoke, fire and revealed figures) and never reads or fills the engine's `visCache`, so monsters' targeting is byte-identical with it on or off (`HEARING.enabled`; a test and the sim confirm the same RNG and monster state). Footsteps are recorded in the shared `walk` (monsters and crews), loud sounds through `makeNoise`'s new `what` (enemy call sites only) plus hearing-only radii where the engine made no noise before: enemy strikes (clash 2), an unseen attacker downing a hero (scream 3), a locked door forced (crash 4), monsters and crew members hurt (scream 2). Entries are deduplicated per hex and kind. The log groups by sound and compass direction ("beyond the door" when a closed door lies on the straight line from the nearest hero), loud first, two lines at most. Stale shove/drag arrows: drawn only while the victim stands next to the landing hex.
- **2026-10-26** V3.9.4: move-risk tags and the backstab dagger fade in (opacity only). They are placed by `transform` every frame, so an animated transform slid them in from the screen's corner; any tag placed that way must not animate `transform` or `scale`.

- **2026-10-26** V4.0 design heuristic (owner): don't overwhelm players with numbers. The mechanics stay as deep as they are, but be strategic about which numbers are shown and when: immediately visible numbers are limited to what is relevant to making an informed choice; the rest lives behind hover. Recorded as design pillar 15. First applied to the pre-delve (Lowgate) UI overhaul.
