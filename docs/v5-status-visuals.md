# V5.0 status visuals: what each status must say, and where

Play-test (27 Oct): "the black ring for darkness isn't clear; poison, and status effects in general, need much more visual emphasis." This is the play track's spec for every status and stance in the game. P builds the HUD side (party cards, the figure tags' content, the log); G builds the in-world side in the new look (`docs/v5-graphics-plan.md`, W1 and W2) from the "In world" column here. Owner gate: the pitch's P6 (status visuals) before anything is built.

Pillars: 11 (visual clarity, with intention: once seen, say what matters and what you can do), 15 (numbers only where they inform a choice: a bleed count and a torch's rounds are shown; a ward's rounds live on hover), 16 (a rule you can guess from what you see), 3 (For the King level of information, no clutter).

## Principles

1. **Three channels, one message.** Every status reads the same way in three places: the **figure** (in world: G), its **tag** (the little strip over the figure: P's content, G's placement and style), and the **party card** (the HUD: P). The figure says *that* something is wrong at a glance across the room; the tag says *what*; the card says *what to do about it* (hover) and carries the number that decides.
2. **Priority sets the volume.** Priority 1 statuses must read from across the board at any zoom (the figure changes shape, colour or motion). Priority 2 must read when the figure is in view (a clear mark on the figure, the tag). Priority 3 is a tag or card detail only.
3. **One figure, at most one loud thing.** When two priority-1 statuses stack (downed and poisoned; dark and hunted), the figure shows the one that kills sooner; the tag shows both. Order: downed > carried off > poisoned > held / webbed / rooted > dark (on Level 3, dark outranks poison: the Gloam hits for 4).
4. **Colour has meaning and stays put.** Poison is green, bleeding is red, darkness is desaturation and cold blue, fear is pale, stealth and veils are a dark violet shimmer, guard and brace are a warm gold edge, fire is orange. Never reuse a status colour for something else in the scene.
5. **Nothing the party hasn't seen** (pillar 11): monster moods, poison on monsters and a crew member's state show only on figures in sight, as today.
6. **Hover explains everything** (the card icons and tags already do; keep every new mark hoverable or paired with a card line).
7. **The log names the change** ("Fenna is poisoned.", "The dark closes on Keven."); the status's first appearance in a delve also gets a one-line coach tip if the coach is on.

## Legend for the tables

- **Read:** what the player must know in a glance and what decision it informs.
- **Pri:** 1 reads across the room; 2 reads in view; 3 tag or card only.
- **Card:** the party card (hud.ts `statusIcons`, the card classes `dark`, `poisoned`, `done`).
- **Tag:** the strip over the figure (today diorama.ts `heroTag`, `monsterTag`, `venomMark`, `bleedMark`; see "Where the tag content lives" below).
- **In world:** the ask for G: the figure, its hex and its light, in the new look.
- **Log:** the line in the chronicle, if any.

## Crew members (only when seen; they play by the party's rules, pillar 17)

The same marks as heroes for the same states: downed with a bleed count (`✚ N`), carried (by a crewmate), poisoned, rooted, feared, braced (crews brace, 31.9), veiled, on overwatch, and carrying a hero (the red hand + direction, see Carried off). Their stance (waiting, demanding, hostile, hired, fleeing) is the crew flag on the tag today; keep it, and give a hostile crew member the same thin red hex ring a hunting monster has, because the same free-swing rule applies.

## Where the tag content lives (a request to G)

The tag's *content* (`heroTag`, `monsterTag`, `venomMark`, `bleedMark`) is in `src/view/diorama.ts`, which G owns. For P to build the tag side of this spec without touching the diorama, G could move those four functions (and `pipsHtml`) to a new P-owned module `src/ui/tags.ts` and import them; the diorama keeps the placement, the styling and the `unit-tag` element. Until then, P specs the content here and G applies it. Noted under "View needs" in `docs/v5-plan.md`.

## HUD side: what P builds once the pitch is approved

1. Card icons split and ordered by priority: Braced, Guarding/Guarded, Overwatch and Veiled each get their own icon (today they share "Protected" and "Also"); the dark card gets the cold wash; the poisoned card the green HP stripe; the downed card the red edge and the big count.
2. Tags: the `unlit` chip (and "the dark hunts" on L3), `rooted · N`, `webbed`, `held`, `dread`, `z` on dozing heroes, `braced`, `guards X`/`guarded`, `overwatch`, `veiled`, `burning`, the torch flame with rounds under 5, "−1" beside the poison drop; the carrier's "carrying X"; the dragged hero's red hand.
3. Log and coach: the first-time lines above ("The dark closes on Keven.", "Brann braces.", "Rae is on overwatch."), each once per delve, and a coach tip on a status's first appearance.
4. Numbers (pillar 15): shown in view only for the bleed count, the torch's rounds, the rooted rounds, and poison's −1; everything else on hover.
