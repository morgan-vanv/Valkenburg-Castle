"Today" uses the live game's numbers (src/view/world.ts, camera.ts, unit.ts). The FTK numbers are this study's proposal, read off reference screenshots; IronOak has not published its camera or model specs.

What to look for

  • Today's art, FTK camera shows the real problem: the camera alone gets the framing, but at that distance the primitives, flat floor and empty room fall apart.
  • FTK board shrinks heroes against the tile, gives them big heads, bedrolls and gear, and fills the room. Scale comes from density as much as from size.
  • Zoomed out checks that the same art still reads when you pull back to plan a move, so free zoom works without a separate planning view.
  • V5.0 default is the owner's chosen starting camera (distance 16, FOV 30); the pitch of 48° is a starting guess that eases lower as you zoom in.
  • Everything here is built from primitives in the browser with no downloads, on three.js r170 with its own post-processing. Real art would come from authored or CC0 models.
